# Loading-screen tips, one to a line, shown in a shuffled order. Lines starting with # are skipped.
# Each must be true for the current code. Another language goes in <code>.txt beside this file.
Villagers pin fresh notes on their village's Quest Board each day, and untaken notes come down at dawn.
Hunt notes name a monster, Clear notes count any monster near the board, and Fetch notes want goods.
Every contract has a deadline, and an expired one pays nothing. Keep an eye on the days left.
Use a finished contract on a Quest Board to be paid in Guild Marks.
Deliveries and escorts appear once a village has a Trade Road. Escorts often meet bandits on the way.
Press H to show or hide the contract tracker, with days left and an arrow to your goal.
Where a Quest Board stands, a villager may become a Guildmaster, selling gear and relic caches for Marks.
Contracts earn guild XP, and every guild level brings a perk point. Press G to spend it in the Ledger.
Notes are ranked F to S, like you. You can take any note up to one rank above your own.
To climb a rank, reach the guild level it needs, then pass a trial from a Guild Hall receptionist.
Villages remember you, from Stranger up to Hero. Higher standing adds extra Marks to their contracts.
Sneak and use a Quest Board to see the village's Advances, the projects your contracts help build.
Contracts that suit a village's current project count triple toward it.
Coaches run along finished Trade Roads. Use /coach in a village with one, or click a wheel on the map.
Press M for the world map. It fills in as you explore, and right-click drops a named pin.
Villages, lairs and troll camps go on your map and compass once you come near them.
Press K for your character sheet, and hover any line to see what it means.
With a sword or axe, click to chain up to three attacks. Sneak and click for a heavy blow you can charge.
Raise your shield just as a blow lands to parry it. Strike the reeling foe for a riposte.
Block a blow with your shield, then click at once for a guard counter.
Press Left Alt to dodge roll. Blows, arrows and blasts can't touch you mid-roll.
Press R for your weapon skill. It spends focus, which comes back faster with every blow you land.
Press the middle mouse button to lock on. Flick the mouse sideways to switch targets.
Attacks, rolls, blocks and sprinting in a fight all cost stamina. Ease off a moment to refill it.
Catch a foe facing away from you and your light attack becomes a backstab.
Look for cold Wardfires near villages and on the way into lairs. Kindle one to put it on your map.
The first Wardfire you kindle gives you the Warden's Flask. Press V to drink from it.
Rest at a lit Wardfire to heal, refill your flask and make it the place you wake if you fall.
Click a Wardfire you've kindled on the world map to travel there. Following guild members come too.
Fall carrying Guild Marks and they stay behind. Reach them before you fall again to get them back.
Most lairs are dungeons dug underground, with a cold Wardfire waiting in the entrance hall.
Use the mist at a boss hall's door to step through. It won't let you out while its master lives.
Break the pots in a dungeon. Some hold Guild Marks.
Lair notes from D rank up may lead to the Warden's Watch, where an old knight still keeps his post.
Hill Trolls sleep by their fires by day. Sneak close and your first melee blow lands twice as hard.
Some nights, far out in the wild, a horn sounds in the dark. A Dusk Rider is coming for you.
A Dusk Rider fades at dawn. Bring it down before then and it leaves its Dusk Bridle behind.
Capital cities are walled and guarded, and no monsters spawn inside their walls.
Villages you build up may be besieged at night. Break every wave before dawn to win.
Rival guilds want villages too. Beat a rival's captain at the village bell to take their village.
Wren, Tobin, Ilsa and Pip may seek you out once a village knows your name.
At rank E you can found a guild at a Guild Hall desk, then recruit villagers where you're trusted.
Raise your guild's banner over a village and it pays you tribute when you visit.
Each village holds a festival one day in seven, and its contracts pay a quarter more that day.
Milestones earn titles. Type /titles to wear one after your name.
